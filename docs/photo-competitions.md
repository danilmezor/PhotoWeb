# Photography Competitions — Master List

_Compiled 2026-06-09 from three adversarially-verified deep-research runs. For an amateur/semi-pro **landscape / travel / nature / outdoor** photographer based in the US, shooting the American West (JMT, PCT, Grand Canyon Rim-to-Rim, Death Valley, Sierra Nevada, Zion, hiking, ultralight). Goal: free/low-cost entry, real credibility + third-party press, **no rights grabs**._

> ⚠️ **Two standing caveats.** (1) All fees, deadlines, prize amounts, categories, and eligibility are year-specific and **change annually — confirm on the official site before entering.** (2) Rights/copyright terms were only *verified* for the competitions explicitly marked "clean" or "rights grab" below; for the rest, **read the terms clause yourself** before submitting.

---

## Why this matters (the strategy link)

Awards are the highest-leverage, **monthly-cadence-friendly** discovery channel for this photographer: they need only a handful of strong images per year, confer real credibility, and — crucially — winning/shortlisting generates **third-party PRESS coverage** (PetaPixel, My Modern Met, Digital Camera World, CNN), which is the *relevant editorial backlink* that actually compounds owned-domain SEO. The awards' own results pages usually **do not** link to your site; the press coverage is the funnel. See `seo-discovery-strategy.md`.

---

## How to read a competition before entering (rights-grab checklist)

The terms clause is where contests bite. Compare every contest's language against these:

- ✅ **Acceptable (model = TPOTY):** *"does not make any claim on the copyright of your images"*; reserved use *"only in connection with these awards."* Photographer keeps copyright; org's license is narrow + award-related.
- 🚫 **Rights grab:** broad license that is **perpetual / irrevocable / royalty-free / worldwide / transferable / sublicensable / covers derivative works** for general promotion or any purpose. You may still "retain copyright" while granting away everything that matters.
- 🚫 **Vanity / pay-to-play:** paid "guaranteed inclusion / top photographer catalogue" (~$2k). Legit comps charge modest *entry* fees or pay included artists. (A high fee alone ≠ scam — judge legitimacy, not just price.)
- 📌 **Precedent:** even **National Geographic** once ran contest terms that claimed broad rights and **backed off under criticism** — big brand ≠ safe terms. Always read.

## 🚫 AVOID — verified rights grabs

| Competition | Why avoid |
|---|---|
| **PCTA photo contest** | Perfect subject fit (PCT/JMT) **but** entering grants a *royalty-free, perpetual, worldwide, **sublicensable** license* (reproduce/distribute/transform/display, any media/size; may hand photos to other orgs). You keep copyright, but it's a broad grab. Only enter if you accept this. |
| **Share the Experience** (US federal public-lands) | Free + amateur-friendly **but** Official Rules PDF imposes a *nonexclusive, worldwide, royalty-free, **perpetual** license to edit/adapt/make derivative works/broadcast/publish* for promotion, "in perpetuity without compensation, notification, or permission." (Earlier guidance that called this a "top free pick" was based only on its /guidelines page — **corrected** after reading the full rules.) |
| **Analog Sparks** | ASMP flags it as a textbook *"irrevocable, perpetual, unlimited, royalty-free, transferable, infinitely sub-licensable"* grab. (Film-only — not your medium; listed as the benchmark for bad terms.) |
| **Paid "catalogue inclusion" / "top contemporary photographer" schemes** (~$2k) | Vanity pay-to-play; no real recognition. |

## Recommended shortlist + annual calendar

Cleanest free/credible core, in rough order through the year (verify dates each cycle):

1. **Apr 15 – May 31:** NLPA (aesthetic fit; naturalistic landscape) — *paid, reputable*
2. **~Jun 8:** NPOTY — *paid, reputable*
3. **~Early Oct:** ILPOTY — *paid, reputable*
4. **~Oct 12:** TPOTY (cleanest terms; travel/hiking) — *paid, free youth route*
5. **~Early Dec:** Wildlife POTY — **FREE if ≤26**, clean terms (enter via Natural Artistry / Bigger Picture)
6. **~Early Jan:** Sony WPA — **FREE**, clean; enter every year regardless
7. **~Spring:** BigPicture — cheap, on-subject (Landscapes/Waterscapes/Flora), SF museum exhibition

**Zero-downside starting moves:** the two free, clean ones — **Sony WPA** and (if ≤26) **Wildlife POTY** — then add **BigPicture** (cheap, on-subject) and 1–2 paid landscape awards (**NLPA + ILPOTY**) where your strongest single frames have a real shot. A single win/shortlist → press → editorial backlinks → compounding owned-site SEO.
